# Reviewer Personas

Seven review perspectives used by `/prd-challenge` (as its four-risks lens) and `/strategy-sprint`.

**Read this when:** You are running a multi-perspective document review.

## Contents

### Files

- [customer-voice.md](customer-voice.md) — Evaluate from the customer's perspective
- [designer-reviewer.md](designer-reviewer.md) — Adopt a product design perspective to review specs, features, and user experiences
- [engineer-reviewer.md](engineer-reviewer.md) — Adopt an engineering perspective to review product specs, PRDs, and technical proposals
- [executive-reviewer.md](executive-reviewer.md) — Review specs and proposals from a strategic business perspective
- [legal-advisor.md](legal-advisor.md) — Flag legal, compliance, and regulatory concerns early
- [skeptic.md](skeptic.md) — Challenge assumptions and play devil's advocate
- [uxr-analyst.md](uxr-analyst.md) — Analyze from user research perspective and identify gaps
