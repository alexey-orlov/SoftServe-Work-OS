# Strategy

Roadmaps, business context, and the strategy frameworks the team reasons with.

**Read this when:** You need to know where the product is going or why.

## Contents

### Subfolders

- [business-context/](business-context/) — Company, product, market, and business-model fundamentals. Needed well beyond strategy work — PRDs, pricing, positioning, metrics, and customer work all depend on it
- [frameworks/](frameworks/) — Reference frameworks the team uses to structure strategic thinking. Stable references — not living docs
- [roadmaps/](roadmaps/) — Time-boxed roadmaps by quarter or half. Long-range vision and narrative docs live here too

### Files

- [current-quarter.md](current-quarter.md) — This quarter's objectives, key results, in-flight initiatives, and the explicit "not doing" list. Injected by the session-start hook, so keep it to one screen
- [okr-guide.md](okr-guide.md) — How we write OKRs: good objectives vs bad, laddering to company OKRs, the 70% rule. Read when refreshing current-quarter.md
