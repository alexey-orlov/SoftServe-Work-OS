# Feature-Request Triage Board

The standing act / decline / later board `/prioritize-requests` maintains — one row per
themed cluster of customer requests, with the verdict and its reasoning. Individual
request records (evidence, draft tickets, tracker state) live in
`product/user-insights/feature-requests/`; this page is the decision layer over them.

_updated: 2026-08-30_

**Read this when:** You want to know what the team decided about a requested capability,
or you're about to prioritize new requests (`/prioritize-requests` reads and updates this
board).

## How rows work

- One row per theme, newest verdicts at the top of their section.
- Verdicts: **Act now** (routed to an initiative — linked), **Later** (parked with a
  revisit trigger), **Declined** (with the reason customers get).
- Every row cites its evidence: the request records and interview counts behind it.
