# Launches

Filled launch checklists and gate verdicts, keyed by INITIATIVE — launches are launches of initiatives (`{initiative-slug}-launch-checklist.md` by `/launch-checklist`; `{initiative-slug}-gate-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` by `/feature-launch-gate`, which on PASS also flips the initiative to `shipped` and the targeted features to `live` in the catalog). Both files of one launch sort together; each carries `initiatives:` frontmatter. Checklists start from [../handbook/templates/launch-checklist-template.md](../handbook/templates/launch-checklist-template.md).

**Read this when:** You are planning a launch and want to learn from past ones, or checking a launch's status.
