# Handbook

How we work: blank templates, team writing conventions, and the canonical de-risk chain. Reference material only — records of work live elsewhere (filled launch checklists in [../launches/](../launches/), rollups in [../reports/](../reports/)).

**Read this when:** You are doing a recurring task and want the team's agreed way of doing it.

One deliberate exception to "reference lives with its domain": templates are single-domain but centralize here as a governed registry — they are stamped out by skills rather than read in place, and the copy-don't-edit and placeholder-exemption rules key on this one path prefix. Domain-bound guides (`okr-guide.md`, `roadmap-guide.md`) stay with their domain in `../strategy/`.
