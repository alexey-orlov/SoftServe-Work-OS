# Planning

Forward plans — the daily plan and the week's priorities. Plans are inputs to the day, not
meeting records; `/weekly-review` reads them back for plan-vs-actual.

**Read this when:** You want today's plan, this week's priorities, or what was planned
against what actually happened.

Two writers, told apart by filename prefix (registry: `governance/write-back-contract.md`, "One writer per surface"):

| Pattern | Writer |
|---------|--------|
| `{YYYY-MM-DD}-daily-plan.md` (+ `{YYYY-MM-DD}-draft.md` end-of-day drafts) | `/daily-plan` |
| `{YYYY}-W{XX}-weekly-plan.md` | `/weekly-plan` |

Several people planning in one repo? Put your handle in the filename:
`{YYYY-MM-DD}-{handle}-daily-plan.md`.
