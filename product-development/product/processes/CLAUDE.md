# Processes

Blank templates, writing conventions, and launch records.

**Read this when:** You are doing a recurring task and want the team's agreed way of doing it.

## Contents

### Subfolders

- [launches/](launches/) — Filled launch checklists, one per feature. `/launch-checklist` writes here
- [templates/](templates/) — Blank templates with bracketed placeholders. Copy, do not edit in place
- [writing-guides/](writing-guides/) — Team writing conventions by audience

### Files

- [de-risk-a-bet.md](de-risk-a-bet.md) — The canonical skill chain for stress-testing a bet from idea to ship (/assumption-map → /prd-draft → /red-team → /prd-review-panel → /pre-mortem → /launch-checklist → /feature-launch-gate), with skip rules and feedback loops
