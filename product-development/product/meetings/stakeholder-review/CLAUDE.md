# Stakeholder Reviews

Work presented to stakeholders for feedback, approval, or sign-off — steering presentations, gate reviews, demo-and-feedback sessions, exec reviews.

**Read this when:** You need what stakeholders said, what was approved or pushed back on, and what was committed to.

## Contents

### Subfolders

- [docs/](docs/) — Agendas and prepared notes
- [summaries/](summaries/) — Condensed summaries. Read these first
- [transcripts/](transcripts/) — Raw transcripts. Open only when the summary is insufficient
