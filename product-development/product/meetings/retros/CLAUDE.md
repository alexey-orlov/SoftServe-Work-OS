# Retros

Retrospective writeups and rolling lessons learned.

**Read this when:** You are running a retro or looking for a recurring theme.

> Unlike the sibling meeting folders, retros are flat — a retro produces a writeup, not a transcript/summary pair. `lessons-learned.md` is the rolling lessons file; dated files hold individual retro writeups; raw recordings live in `transcripts/`.

## Contents

### Files

- [lessons-learned.md](lessons-learned.md) — Rolling team-process lessons (`- YYYY-MM-DD — lesson (source retro link)`); appended by `/process-meeting`, `/weekly-review`, and `/context-update`. Ships with one synthetic example entry

### Subfolders

- [transcripts/](transcripts/) — Raw retro recordings; open only when the writeup is insufficient

_Add a one-line entry here for every retro writeup you add._
