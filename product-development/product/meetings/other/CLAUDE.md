# Other Meetings

The catch-all — meetings no series or event type fits: all-hands, vendor calls, trainings, one-off cross-team syncs.

**Read this when:** You are tracing a meeting that fits no other folder. The same meeting landing here twice → promote it to its own series (procedure in [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)).

## Contents

### Subfolders

- [docs/](docs/) — Agendas and prepared notes
- [summaries/](summaries/) — Condensed summaries. Read these first
- [transcripts/](transcripts/) — Raw transcripts. Open only when the summary is insufficient
